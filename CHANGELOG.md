# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First complete version.

- **Check-in:** mark Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha as with congregation, alone or missed; long-press to clear.
- **History:** month calendar coloured by congregation count; tap any past day to correct it.
- **Stats:** current and best streak (target of 3, 4 or 5 congregation prayers per day), a 7-day chart, week and 30-day summaries.
- **Prayer times:** Aladhan API for the chosen city, calculation method and Asr school, cached for offline use.
- **Reminders:** local notifications at prayer time or 5, 10 or 15 minutes before, in English or Urdu.
- **Languages and theme:** English and Urdu with right-to-left layout; system, light or dark theme.
- **Privacy:** no account and no tracking; everything is stored on the device.
